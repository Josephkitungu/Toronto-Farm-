ABOUT US

Toronto Farm commenced its operations in Taita Taveta County, Voi on 18/7/2020 with a focus in solving hunger crisis in the region under the leadership of its Founder and CEO Joe Kitungu. Its main focus was to provide fresh quality farm produce in the region. The Farm Currently deals with Poulty Farming, Apiculture, Dairy Farming, Vegetable Farming, Beef Farming and also we do Tobacco Farming

Why Us?

1.Organic Farming Practices Unlike many farms that rely heavily on synthetic fertilizers and chemical pesticides, Toronto Farm is committed to organic methods. This means healthier produce for consumers, better soil health over time, and a lower environmental footprint.

2.Greenhouse Technology The farm uses greenhouse cultivation, which allows for controlled growing conditions — protecting crops from unpredictable weather, extending growing seasons, and improving yield consistency compared to farms that depend entirely on open-field, rain-fed agriculture.

3.Local Job Creation Toronto Farm isn't just about production — it's an economic engine for the Taita Taveta community. By creating employment opportunities, the farm contributes directly to local livelihoods rather than operating as an isolated commercial venture.

4. Community Investment Through Donations Beyond employment, the farm gives back through donations, reflecting a business model built on shared prosperity rather than profit alone. This positions Toronto Farm as a socially responsible enterprise, not just an agricultural business.

Social Responsibility

Toronto Farm Inc. has managed to help neighbours by assisting in Land reclamation and also providing a ready market for them when selling their lands as well as providing job opportunities. We are also engaged in periodic donations to Churches, Schools, Mosques and during food crisis periods especially to people affected by Social Calamities. We also run Toronto Farm Foundation which was established in 2024 to help needy students to access to better education. over that period we have sponsored about 200+ Students to achieve their dreams.Toronto Foundation has also contributed to building over 30 schools in the regions we are locted as well as taking part in climate conservation by planting over 6M trees in Semi-Arid Area.
